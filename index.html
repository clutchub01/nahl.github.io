Study plans

A timetable that survives a real week.

Tell Nahl when you sleep, when your head is clearest and what is due. It builds a day-by-day plan around the hours you actually have, then rebuilds it when the week falls apart.

PDF to flashcards

Your lecture slides, quizzable in seconds.

Drop in the PDF. Nahl pulls out the ideas worth testing and builds a deck with spaced repetition already wired in, so your review lands on the day you are about to forget.

A 40-page slide deck becomes a 25-minute review, not a lost evening.
