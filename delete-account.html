Delete your account

Nahl · Lunnovate Group

This page explains how to permanently delete your Nahl account and the study data attached to it. You can do it from inside the app, or by email if the app is no longer installed.

From inside the app

  1. Open Nahl and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and cloud study records are erased immediately.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address registered to your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We may ask you to confirm the request before deleting.

Email the deletion request

We permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your account and profile, flashcards, courses, timetables, mock test results, course grades, and focus session statistics.

Purchase records required for financial or tax compliance may be retained in anonymized form as required by law.
