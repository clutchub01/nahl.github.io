Lunnovate Group ("we," "our," or "us") built Nahl as a mobile and web study operating system for students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and delete your information when you use the Nahl mobile application and website, in compliance with Google Play User Data policies and applicable data protection laws (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act).

1. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Email Address & User ID: Collected when you create an account or sign in via email or Google Sign-In to sync your study progress across devices.
  • Display Name & Academic Preferences: Optional details you enter, such as your institution, level, courses, and study targets.

B. User-Generated Study Content

  • Decks, Notes, Courses & Timetables: Flashcards, CBT mock test results, uploaded study notes or outlines, course grades, and Pomodoro focus session statistics you create inside Nahl.

C. Device & App Functionality Data

  • Push Notification Tokens: Collected via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send study reminders, timetable alerts, and streak notifications you opt into.
  • Foreground App Package Name (On-Device Only): When you explicitly enable Nahl's Pomodoro App Blocker via Android's AccessibilityService API, Nahl checks which app is currently open in the foreground solely to block distracting apps you selected during an active focus session. This data is processed strictly in real time on your device—it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

D. Subscription & Purchase History

  • Billing Status: When you subscribe to Nahl Pro (Dean's List) via Google Play Billing (managed via RevenueCat) or Paystack (on web), we receive confirmation of your subscription status, plan tier, and expiration date. We do not collect or store your full credit or debit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  1. Provide, sync, and back up your flashcards, courses, timetables, and study streaks.
  2. Generate AI flashcards, summaries, and practice tests from the study materials you submit.
  3. Enforce your chosen app-blocking rules during active Pomodoro focus timers.
  4. Process and verify Nahl Pro subscriptions and restore purchases across devices.
  5. Deliver scheduled study reminders and essential service updates.

We never sell your personal data or show third-party behavioral ads based on your study materials.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Nahl relies on trusted third-party infrastructure providers to operate core app features:

  • Supabase: Authentication, cloud database storage, and serverless backend functions.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: In-app subscription management and purchase verification on Android.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Delivery of push notifications and study reminders.
  • AI Inference Providers (Groq / Llama): Processing user-submitted study notes to generate flashcards and practice questions. Submitted text is not used to train public models.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the Nahl app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption. Access to user records is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies so only your authenticated account can access your private study data.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and study data for as long as your account remains active. You may delete your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • Inside the app: Go to Profile / Settings → Delete Account to permanently erase your account and cloud study records immediately.
  • Via web request: If you no longer have the app installed, use our account deletion page or email lunnovate@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

Purchase records required for financial or tax compliance may be retained in anonymized form as required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Nahl is designed for secondary school and university students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at lunnovate@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
